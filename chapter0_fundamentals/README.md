## 📌 Overview

The material in this chapter covers the core grounding in deep learning fundamentals necessary to complete the more advanced sections of the ARENA curriculum (such as transformers, mechanistic interpretability, reinforcement learning, and generative models).

### ✨ Chapter Highlights
- **Convolution Operations:** Building custom 1D and 2D convolution functions from scratch.
- **ResNets & Classification:** Constructing and loading weights into a Residual Neural Network, then fine-tuning it on classification tasks.
- **Optimization:** Hyperparameter tuning and tracking using Weights & Biases (`wandb`).
- **Autograd:** Implementing a custom backpropagation engine from scratch.
- **Generative Modeling:** Constructing and training Variational Autoencoders (VAEs) and Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) to generate images.
